\subsection{Simple ShearBand}\label{ss:simple_shearband}

The simple shear band problem consists of a box mesh put into pure shear. Periodic boundary conditions exist at $x=0$ and $x=0$ for $u_x$ and $u_y$. The remeshing is set up so that the grid structure of the original mesh is preserved, with the same number of elements in the $x$ and $y$ directions, shown in Figure~\ref{fig:mesh_ex}. 

A vertical line sample of the effective plastic strain is shown in Figure~\ref{fig:field_ex}. The field at the restart step~(before any solve) is shown to verity the accuracy of the L2 projection. Figure~\ref{fig:field_ex}(b) shows the fields after a solve, demonstrating consistency after a the solve. 

Figure~\ref{fig:ssb_accel_vel} show the states of acceleration and velocity for the restart step and the step after. The velocity curves remain consistent with each other, while a clear divergence is seen in the accelerations. This is largely due to slight errors in the velocity recover. Accelerations are calculated with:
\begin{equation}
    a_n = \frac{1}{\beta}\left[\frac{u_n-u_{n-1}}{\Delta t^2}-\frac{v_{n-1}}{\Delta t}-(0.5-\beta)a_{n-1}\right]
\end{equation}

The term $\frac{v_{n-1}}{\Delta t}$ is the key here. Any error in the $L_2$ project of the velocity will scale with $1/\Delta t$. The error becomes pronounced in high gradient areas, like seen around/across the shear band in this problem. Mesh refinement heavily reduces this error.

\subsection{Sandia Fracture Challenge}\label{ss:sandia_fracture_challenge}

I simulated a Sandia fracture challenge using RaM. The domain is in Figure~\ref{fig:challenge_domain}. Loading is done via a point load in the center of the loading pins. The residual value of the degradation is high, $\eta=\num{1e-4}$ to help reduce node movement within a damage band on a restart. 

Figure~\ref{fig:crack_prop} shows the crack propagation through the sample. Figure~\ref{fig:sfc_reactiondisp} shows the reaction vs displacement force. The remapped simulation accurately predicts the two crack nucleation events. There is some discrepancy that forms as the crack grows. Most likely this is due to needing more mesh refinement.
